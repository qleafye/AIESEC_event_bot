Fix payment UX so delegates who have NOT yet paid can actually find where/how to pay, instead of being blocked by a button and messages that only talk about "uploading a receipt". Purpose: Users who haven't paid don't tap a button labeled "upload receipt" — they need a button and messages that say "payment" and show them the card details up front.
Output: Renamed button (in sync across the keyboard builder and its handler filter), requisites shown in both the pay-later message and the multi-option picker, and every stale reference to the old label swept from the codebase.
</objective>
