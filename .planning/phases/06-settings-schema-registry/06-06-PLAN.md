<objective>
Migrate the behavioral feature-switch gates in handlers/registration.py, handlers/payment.py, and services/scheduler.py from the hardcoded `get_setting(k) or "<default>"` / `== "on"` idiom to `get_setting_typed`, completing the locked-decision requirement that the toggle wave absorb every hardcoded feature-switch read — byte-for-byte (REG-02, SC#2). Includes the `_decide_status` approval reads (all four, incl. `full_approval`) and the `_is_module_enabled` consent/payment module gate.

Purpose: These gates decide whether questions appear, whether the party fork shows, a delegate's approval status, whether the consent/payment steps are offered, and whether reminders fire — the highest-stakes reads in the app. Centralizing them on the registry finishes the "one file" promise and removes the last hardcoded defaults. Byte-for-byte preservation is enforced per key with an explicit test each.
Output: migrated gates + a behavior-preservation test for every affected key.
</objective>
